3. Information You Provide

Account Information

To use the App you create an account. Depending on how you sign up, we receive:

  • Email and password sign-up — your email address, your name, and a password. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are stored only in hashed form; we never see or store your password in readable form.
  • Sign in with Google — your email address and name, as released to us by Google when you choose this option. We do not receive your Google password, and we do not request access to your Gmail, Google Drive, contacts, or any other Google service.

Household Content

The App stores the content you create: tasks, task names and notes, categories, icons, frequency settings, completion history, household names, and household membership. We treat this as your content. We use it to operate the App's features and we do not analyze it for advertising or sell it.

4. Information Collected Automatically

Diagnostics

The App uses Sentry to report crashes and performance problems. A diagnostic report may include the type of error, the sequence of code that produced it, your device model, and your iOS version. These reports are configured so that they are not linked to your identity:

  • Your IP address is not collected.
  • No user account identifier is attached to diagnostic reports.
  • Screenshots and screen contents are never captured or transmitted.
  • Account and household identifiers are stripped from network diagnostics before a report is sent.

Diagnostic reports do include a random identifier generated when the App is installed, so that we can tell whether one crash affected many people or one person many times. It is not Apple's advertising identifier, it is not tied to your account, and it is regenerated if you delete and reinstall the App.

On devices running the released version of the App, performance measurements are sampled from a portion of sessions rather than collected from every session, to limit battery and data use.

What We Do Not Collect

The App does not collect your location, contacts, photos, calendars, health or fitness data, financial information, browsing history, or search history. It contains no advertising identifiers, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party analytics used to profile you.

5. How Your Information Is Shared

With Your Household

Happy Home Task Tracker is built for shared households. When you join or invite someone to a household, the members of that household can see the shared content in it, including tasks, notes, completion history, and the name and email address associated with each member. Choose your household members accordingly.

With Service Providers

We use a small number of providers to run the App. They process data on our behalf, under contract, and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes:

  • Supabase — hosts our database and handles account authentication.
  • Google — provides the optional Sign in with Google authentication method.
  • Sentry — receives crash and performance diagnostics.
  • Apple — distributes the App and, if you download it from the App Store, provides us with aggregate statistics that do not identify you.

We Do Not Sell Your Data

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We may disclose information if we are legally required to do so, or where necessary to protect our rights, safety, or the safety of others.

6. Notifications

Task reminders are scheduled and delivered locally by your device. The App does not register for remote push notifications and does not send a push token to our servers. You can turn reminders off at any time in the App or in your device's Settings.

7. How We Protect Your Information

Data travels between the App and our servers over encrypted connections. Access to household data is enforced at the database level, so that records belonging to one household cannot be read by members of another. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security; please see the data loss disclaimer in our Terms of Service.

8. Data Retention & Deletion

We keep your account information and household content for as long as your account exists.

You can delete your account from within the App, under Manage Account. Deleting your account removes your household and its associated content, and then deletes your account itself. This is permanent and cannot be undone. Content you contributed to a household you do not own may remain with that household, as described in our Terms of Service.

Diagnostic reports are not linked to your account and are therefore not retrievable or deletable by account. They are retained on a limited retention schedule by our diagnostics provider and are automatically discarded when it expires.

9. Your Choices & Rights

  • Access and correction — you can view and edit your name, your household content, and your account details directly in the App.
  • Deletion — you can delete your account and its content in the App, as described above.
  • Sign-in method — using Sign in with Google is optional; you may create an account with an email address instead.
  • Notifications — reminders can be disabled at any time.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights over your personal information, such as the right to request a copy of it or to object to certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below and we will respond as required by applicable law.

10. Children's Privacy

If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it. Household members under 18 should use the App only with the involvement of a parent or guardian.
