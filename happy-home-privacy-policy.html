3. Information You Provide

Account Information

To use the App you create an account. Depending on how you sign in, we receive:

  • Email and password — your email address and a password. You choose your name when you create or join a household. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are stored only in hashed form; we never see or store your password in readable form.
  • Sign in with Apple — the email address Apple releases to us, which may be a private Hide My Email relay address rather than your real address, and the name Apple provides. Apple sends that name only the first time you authorize the App, and we save it so we can still show it later. We do not receive your Apple ID password.
  • Sign in with Google — your email address and name, as released to us by Google when you choose this option. We do not receive your Google password, and we do not request access to your Gmail, Google Drive, contacts, or any other Google service.

Sign in with Apple and Sign in with Google are optional. You can create an account with an email address and password instead.

Household Content

The App stores the content you and your household create, and we use it only to operate the App. That content includes:

  • Household name, invite code, and who belongs to the household.
  • Member names and emoji. A person can be added to a household without an account of their own; their name is stored with the household. An email address is stored only when that member has one.
  • Tasks, including names, notes, categories, icons, schedules, due dates, reminders, who is assigned, and completion and snooze history.
  • Rotations: the order of household members who take turns at a task.
  • Vacation mode: whether it is on, and the start and end dates.
  • Quick Add lines you save. Typing @name picks people in your household. The line is parsed on your device. The task that results is what we store.

An optional on-device suggestion, using Apple's on-device model, can read a Quick Add line the parser could not. That feature is turned off in the App we ship. While it is off, a Quick Add line is not sent to the model. It is never sent to us, or to anyone else, to produce a suggestion. If we turn the feature on later, it will still run only on the device.

Problem Reports

If you choose Report a Problem under Manage My Account, we store the message you write, your email address, and your account identifier so we can look into it. The form shows the email address that will be sent. We keep the report until you delete your account. Sending a report is optional and is not required to use the App.

Happy Home Plus

Happy Home Plus is an optional auto-renewing subscription sold by Apple: a monthly plan, a yearly plan, and a seven-day free trial on either plan. A subscription can be shared through Apple Family Sharing. The App reads your subscription status from Apple — which plan you have, whether you are in the trial, when it renews or expires, and whether it is family-shared — so it can unlock Plus for your household. We do not receive your payment card number, and we do not keep a separate purchase ledger on our servers. The product identifier, and whether the subscription is a trial or family-shared, may appear in a diagnostic log.

4. Information Collected Automatically

Diagnostics

The App uses Sentry to report crashes, performance problems, and diagnostic logs. A report may include the type of error, the sequence of code that produced it, your device model, your iOS version, and whether the device is on Wi-Fi or cellular. Crash and performance reports are configured so that they are not linked to your identity:

  • Your IP address is not collected.
  • No account identifier is attached to crash or performance reports.
  • Screenshots and screen contents are never captured or transmitted.
  • Account and household identifiers are stripped from network diagnostics before a report is sent.

Crash reports include a random identifier generated when the App is installed, so that we can tell whether one crash affected many installations or one installation many times. It is not Apple's advertising identifier, it is not tied to your account, and it is regenerated if you delete and reinstall the App.

On devices running the released version of the App, performance measurements are sampled from a portion of sessions rather than collected from every session, to limit battery and data use.

Separate from those reports, the App sends diagnostic logs. Most of them describe what failed without naming you. Some logs include an account, household, or member identifier when that is what the failure is about — for example a push-notification setting that did not save, or an account deletion that did not finish. Those logs are linked to you. They do not include your password, the text of a problem report, or the contents of a Quick Add line.

What We Do Not Collect

The App does not collect your location, contacts, photos, calendars, health or fitness data, browsing history, or search history. It does not collect your payment card number. It contains no advertising identifier, no advertising SDK, and no third-party analytics used to profile you. The home screen widget reads a copy of your tasks stored on the device by the App; the widget does not collect data of its own.

5. How Your Information Is Shared

With Your Household

Happy Home Task Tracker is built for shared households. When you join a household, or someone joins yours with an invite code or link that you share, the members of that household can see the shared content in it, including tasks, notes, completion history, rotations, vacation dates, and the name of each member. A member who has an account can also be seen by their email address. Choose your household members accordingly. We do not email invitations for you; sharing an invite is something you do yourself.

With Service Providers

We use a small number of providers to run the App. They process data on our behalf, under contract, and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes:

  • Supabase — hosts our database and handles account authentication, including Sign in with Apple and Sign in with Google.
  • Apple — distributes the App, processes Sign in with Apple, sells and bills Happy Home Plus, and delivers remote push notifications through the Apple Push Notification service. If you download the App from the App Store, Apple also provides us with aggregate statistics that do not identify you.
  • Google — provides the optional Sign in with Google method. Google Cloud Storage, in the United States (us-east1), stores the encrypted database backups described under Data Retention.
  • Sentry — receives crash reports, performance measurements, and diagnostic logs.

We Do Not Sell Your Data

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We may disclose information if we are legally required to do so, or where necessary to protect our rights, safety, or the safety of others.

6. Notifications

Task reminders you set on a task are scheduled and delivered locally by your device. Those reminders are not sent to our servers.

If you allow notifications, the App also registers with Apple for remote push and sends this device's push token to our servers, stored against your account. We use it to deliver two kinds of notice to other signed-in members of your household: that someone finished a task, and, for a rotating task, that you are up next. The notice names the person and the task. A notice waits briefly so an undo can cancel it, and a notice that is not sent within about an hour is dropped. You can turn each kind of notice off in the App, and you can turn notifications off in your device's Settings. Signing out removes this device's token from your account.

7. How We Protect Your Information

Data travels between the App and our servers over encrypted connections. Access to household data is enforced at the database level, so that records belonging to one household cannot be read by members of another. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security; please see the data loss disclaimer in our Terms of Service.

8. Data Retention & Deletion

We keep your account information, household content, and any problem report you submitted for as long as your account exists.

You can delete your account from within the App, under Manage My Account. If you own the household, deletion removes that household and its tasks, members, and categories, and then deletes your account. If you are a member of a household you do not own, deletion removes your login and your membership; the household and its tasks stay, including content you contributed to it. This is permanent and cannot be undone. If the account was created with Sign in with Apple, the App asks Apple to revoke that sign-in when you delete the account. The push token stored for your account is deleted with the account.

We keep encrypted backups so we can restore the database if it is damaged. Supabase keeps about seven daily backups. A separate encrypted copy is stored in Google Cloud Storage and is kept for 90 days; the backup is encrypted before it is uploaded, and the backup job cannot read it back. Deleting your account removes your information from the live database. A copy can remain in a backup until that backup expires, and we use backups only to recover the service.

Queued push notices are discarded within seven days. Diagnostic reports and logs are retained on a limited schedule by our diagnostics provider. They are not deleted when you delete your account.
