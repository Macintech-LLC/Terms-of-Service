Privacy Policy

ABC SAFESPOT IS AN INTERNAL APPLICATION FOR ACTION BEHAVIOR CENTERS STAFF, DISTRIBUTED ONLY THROUGH THE ORGANIZATION'S MOBILE DEVICE MANAGEMENT SYSTEM. IT SHOWS NO ADVERTISING, DOES NOT TRACK YOU ACROSS OTHER COMPANIES' APPS OR WEBSITES, AND IS NOT AVAILABLE TO THE GENERAL PUBLIC.

1. About This App

ABC SafeSpot (the "App") is an internal mobile application operated by Action Behavior Centers ("we," "us," or "our") for use by its staff on organization-managed devices. The App delivers operational notifications and announcements to managed devices and reports, in aggregate, on whether those announcements are reaching staff.

The App is deployed and configured through Mobile Device Management (MDM). It is not distributed to the general public and cannot be installed outside the organization's managed device fleet.

2. What the App Collects, at a Glance

The table below mirrors the privacy labels shown on the App's App Store listing. "Linked to you" means the information is associated with an identified individual.

Everything the App collects is tied to a device, not to a named person, and is collected for app functionality only. None of it is used for advertising, and none of it is used for tracking as Apple defines that term.

3. Information Collected to Deliver Notifications

  • Push notification token — a token assigned by Apple Push Notification service to the device. It is used solely to deliver notifications from the organization to that device.
  • Device identifier (UDID) — the device's unique identifier, supplied by the MDM system through managed app configuration. It is used to associate the device with the correct notification groups.
  • Usage data — when a notification is opened or the App is used, the App records when the session began, how long it lasted, and whether it started from a notification. These records are associated with the device identifier.

Usage information is reported to administrators only in aggregate. No per-device or per-person activity is displayed.

4. Diagnostics

The App reports crashes and performance problems to Sentry, a diagnostics service, so that faults can be found and fixed. A diagnostic report may include the type of error, the sequence of code that produced it, the device model, and the iOS version. These reports are configured so that they are not linked to any identity:

  • No IP address is collected.
  • No user or account identifier is attached to diagnostic reports.
  • Screenshots and screen contents are never captured or transmitted.
  • The device UDID and the App's managed credentials are removed from a report before it is sent.

Diagnostic reports do include a random identifier generated when the App is installed, so we can tell whether one fault affected many devices or one device many times. It is not Apple's advertising identifier, it is not the device UDID, and it is regenerated if the App is removed and reinstalled.

Performance measurements are sampled from a small portion of sessions rather than collected from every session, to limit battery and data use.

What the App Does Not Collect

The App does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, calendars, health information, financial information, browsing history, or search history. It contains no advertising identifiers and no advertising SDKs. It does not access the contents of any other app on the device.

5. How Information Is Shared

We do not sell your information, we do not share it for advertising, and we do not transfer it to any party for their own purposes. The App relies on a small number of service providers, which process data on our behalf:

  • Apple — the Apple Push Notification service transmits notifications to the device.
  • Vercel — hosts the App's servers and database.
  • Sentry — receives the crash and performance diagnostics described above.

Information may also be disclosed where we are legally required to do so, or where necessary to protect our rights or the safety of others.

6. Data Storage & Retention

Notification tokens and device identifiers are stored on servers hosted by Vercel, and are retained only while the device remains actively enrolled and managed by the organization.

Individual usage records are automatically deleted after 90 days. Aggregate totals, which contain no device identifiers, are retained indefinitely.

Diagnostic reports are not linked to an account and are therefore not retrievable by individual. They are retained on a limited schedule by our diagnostics provider and are discarded automatically when it expires.

7. Managed Devices

The App runs on devices owned and managed by the organization. Device management, enrollment, and the policies applied to a managed device are governed by the organization's own device management practices, which are separate from this policy. Questions about what the organization can see or control on a managed device should be directed to your IT department.

8. Security

Information travels between the App and our servers over encrypted connections. Access to administrative data is restricted to authorized organization personnel. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the App changes. When we do, we will revise the "Last Updated" date above. If the App begins collecting a new category of information, or uses existing information for a materially different purpose, this policy and the App Store privacy labels will be updated together.
